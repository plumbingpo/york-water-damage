Water Extraction

Water Removal for Your Home

A broken pipe, a leaking appliance, or a storm can flood a house fast, and extraction by professionals remains the safest answer and the fastest one. Homeowners in York call York Restoration for extraction jobs of every size, whether it is a damp corner or several inches of standing water. On each job we assess the damage, pump out the water with industrial equipment, map the moisture, protect your belongings, and document everything for your insurer.

Standing water poses a serious risk to your family, your home, and your belongings. It takes only hours for drywall to swell, hardwood to warp, and electrical components to start corroding. If left untreated for 24 to 48 hours, mold growth becomes almost inevitable. Our advice is simple: pick up the phone as soon as you find water pooling indoors, no matter how little there is. A technician will size up how far the water has spread and start removing it before it causes secondary problems.

Hidden leaks that have dripped for days or weeks are part of our work too. Walls and subfloors can hide a small leak well, and the framing may be badly damaged by the time a stain or smell appears. To track down trapped moisture and verify everything is dry, our crew relies on thermal cameras and professional-grade meters.

Structural Drying

Moisture Monitoring and Structural Drying

Complete structural drying is the most critical step in preventing long-term damage to your home after water intrusion. Done right, it pulls trapped moisture out of subfloors, wall cavities, and framing, keeps mold from starting, and returns the structure to sound condition. York Restoration dries every home to the IICRC S500 standard, checking and recording moisture levels each day.

For homes, we bring commercial-grade dehumidifiers and fast air movers, add specialty equipment where hardwood flooring or wall cavities need it, and use thermal imaging to confirm progress along the way. Our technicians look at how severe the damage is, what your home is built from, and how it is laid out, then set the drying plan to fit.

Professional drying goes much further than most people expect. Even after visible water is gone, moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, and in structural framing can fuel mold growth and cause wood rot for weeks. We monitor moisture levels daily and don't consider the job complete until every reading reaches pre-loss standards. Most residential drying projects take 3 to 5 days depending on the extent of damage.

Mold Prevention

Preventing and Removing Mold in Your Home

Within 24 to 48 hours of getting wet, materials can start to grow mold, which spreads quickly and can put your family's health at serious risk. If mold is already growing, York Restoration can remediate it as a standalone service, and prevention is built into each restoration project we do. Our IICRC-certified technicians follow EPA and IICRC S520 protocols for safe, thorough mold treatment.

Any time a home has water damage, our advice is to include a mold prevention treatment. Once mold has settled in, removing it is harder and more expensive than using an antimicrobial treatment during drying. Where growth has already started, seen or suspected after a past water problem, our service covers testing, containing the area, removing the mold, and a follow-up verification that the home is safe.

Sewer Backups

Cleaning Up After a Sewer Backup

When a sewer line backs up into the house, you are dealing with one of the most hazardous water losses there is. The health risks are serious because Category 3 water, also called black water, carries bacteria and viruses along with other pathogens. Contaminated water calls for strict IICRC protocols, and the York Restoration crew has the specialized training and equipment to follow them safely.

The work runs in six steps: sealing off the area, safely pumping out the sewage, hauling away materials beyond saving, antimicrobial cleaning, full structural drying, and a final test confirming the house is safe. We handle the entire process from start to finish so you and your family can return to a clean, healthy home as quickly as possible.
