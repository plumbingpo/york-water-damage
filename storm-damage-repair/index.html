When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in York

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
