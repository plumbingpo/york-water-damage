When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in York

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.

The Benefits of Professional Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Mopping the puddle in front of you is rarely the real challenge during an emergency. The difficulty lies in tracking where the remaining water travelled. Water runs under baseboards, into wall cavities and beneath flooring within minutes. With a thermal imaging camera and moisture meters we locate that hidden water and treat it the same night, before it turns up weeks later as an odor or a stain.

Our night crew arrives with gear homes simply do not have, including commercial air movers, properly sized dehumidifiers and truck-mounted extractors. The crew is trained to stay safe near contaminated water and wet wiring, and from hour one it records the loss in photos and moisture readings. Those records are useful if you file an insurance claim. You get one team that handles the emergency and stays with the job until the home is dry.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
